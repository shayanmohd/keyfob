First release.

Turn one recovery phrase into a set of guardian cards, spread across people and places. Choose how many
cards exist and how many are needed to rebuild it. Fewer than the threshold reveal nothing at all.

Cards are written in BIP-39 words with their own checksum. The plan, the letter and a rehearsal drill are
kept with them, because a recovery plan nobody has practised is a wish.

Not a wallet. No address, no balance, no internet permission.
