First release.

Turn one recovery phrase into a set of guardian cards, spread across people and places. You choose how
many cards exist and how many are needed to rebuild it. Fewer than the threshold reveal nothing at all.

Cards are written in BIP-39 words with their own checksum. The plan, the letter to your heirs and a
rehearsal drill sit alongside them, because a plan nobody has practised is a wish.

Not a wallet. No address, no balance, no internet permission.
